Frequently Asked Questions

self-service

Answers residents can find themselves, before they need to line up.

How do I share feedback or a suggestion with the barangay? +
Head to the Community Forum, choose Feedback or Suggestion, and post — it goes live once a barangay staff member approves it.
Can I file a complaint anonymously? +
Yes — toggle "submit anonymously" in the Complaint Center before sending your report.
How do I track my complaint's status? +
Every complaint gets a reference number you can check anytime under Complaint Center → Track Status.
